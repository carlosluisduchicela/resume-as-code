{ } resume‑as‑code

one resume file. no version ever disagrees.

the problem: your pdf says one job title, your linkedin says another, and the recruiter's system has a third — because you retype the same career into every form. here the resume lives in one structured file. edit it once and the ats text, the readable resume, and the linkedin copy are rebuilt from it. a lint pass catches bad dates, duplicates, and missing fields while you type.

i run my own job search on this system. the sample resume is fictional; the setup is the point.
